The studio

XR · Asturias

Immersive Oasis designs and develops interactive and immersive experiences for Extended Reality (XR) and spatial computing. It was founded in Asturias in 2020 and works with both companies and public institutions.

Its main lines are virtual reality applications, location-based entertainment, mixed reality content and 360-degree video, along with the hardware support that goes with them.

The work splits mainly between healthcare, industry and tourism: medical training and digital therapies for pharmaceutical companies such as Daiichi Sankyo, AstraZeneca, Bayer and Sanofi; simulation and visualisation for industry; and heritage projects with town councils like Oviedo and Aller.

My work there

I am lead developer and CTO at the studio, and sometimes a graphics programmer too. I have worked on every project except the video-only ones.
